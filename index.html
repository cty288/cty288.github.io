About

Systems instincts, AI focus.

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Stanford University, focusing on AI and systems. My recent industry experience includes a backend engineering internship at Stripe, and I will be returning to Stripe's Risk Capabilities group as a full-time engineer. This early experience in systems and framework design continues to influence how I approach engineering challenges today.

I am motivated by problems at the intersection of systems and intelligent systems, and I enjoy building infrastructure and platforms that enable breakthroughs in AI and data-driven applications.

Research / LLM Agents

ReCAP: Recursive Context-Aware Reasoning and Planning for Large Language Model Agents

Zhenyu Zhang, Tianyi Chen, Weiran Xu, Alex Pentland, Jiaxin Pei

arXiv:2510.23822 NeurIPS 2025

Co-developed a hierarchical framework for long-horizon LLM agents that plans ahead, executes the first subtask, recursively decomposes composite work, and re-injects the parent plan on return to keep multi-level context coherent.

  • Combines plan-ahead decomposition, structured parent-plan re-injection, and bounded active prompts.
  • Evaluated under strict pass@1 on Robotouille, ALFWorld, FEVER, and SWE-bench.
